# Instantly Lead Conversion

Converting interested responses into booked meetings. This is where money is made or lost.

## The Speed Rule

**Fastest responders book the most meetings.**

When someone responds interested:
- They're in the mindset NOW
- Tomorrow they've forgotten
- Next week they've moved on

### How to Respond Faster

1. **Schedule inbox checks** — 10-minute slots morning/afternoon/evening
2. **Keep Instantly open** — Tab always visible
3. **Set up Slack notifications** — Settings → Integrations → Slack
4. **Use Zapier** — Get alerts on your phone
5. **Hire a VA** — For response handling if volume is high

## Response Strategy

### Don't Send Calendly Links

**Bad:**
> "Great! Here's my Calendly: [link]"

**Good:**
> "Great! Does Monday at 1pm work for you?"

**Why:** Calendly adds friction. Specific times get more bookings.

If they give a time, book it yourself. Don't make them click another link.

### Be Specific and Tailored

**Lazy response:**
> "Here's the Loom, let me know if you have questions."

**Better response:**
> "Thanks for your interest! Just checked your website — seeing you're focused on [SPECIFIC THING]. I put together a Loom showing exactly how we'd approach [THEIR GOAL] for [THEIR COMPANY]. Here it is: [link]
>
> Based on what I saw, I'd love to try [SPECIFIC TACTIC]. Free tomorrow at 2pm?"

### Custom Looms Convert Better

When someone responds interested:

1. Go to their website
2. Note what they do, who they serve
3. Record a Loom specifically for them
4. Reference their business in the video
5. Show exactly how you'd help THEM

**Example flow:**
- They sell social media management tools
- Go to Lead Finder → Technologies → Shopify
- Show them: "Here's how we'd target Shopify store CEOs for you"
- Reference their existing clients if visible

This extra effort is worth it when one client = $2,500+ lifetime value.

## The CRM Mindset

Use Instantly's CRM view (Deal Flow) instead of just Unibox.

**Add prospect value** — Seeing "$2,000" next to a lead makes you treat them like a $2,000 opportunity, not a random inbox message.

**Add notes** — Track what you discussed, what they need
**Set reminders** — "Follow up if no reply in 1 day"

## Follow-Up Rules

### Keep Following Up Until:
1. They book a call, OR
2. They tell you to stop

That's it. No other reason to stop.

### Follow-Up Frequency

| Stage | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| Initial interest, no reply | Next day |
| Still no reply | Every 2-3 days |
| Gone cold | Weekly for 2-3 weeks |
| Long-term nurture | Monthly |

**People are thankful for follow-ups.** They forget. They get busy. Following up is doing them a favor.

### Out-of-Office Handling

Use subsequences in Instantly:

1. Create subsequence triggered by "Out of Office" status
2. Set delay (e.g., 7 days)
3. Step 1: "Hey, noticed you were out — reaching back now. Still interested in [OFFER]?"
4. Sequence stops when they reply

Instantly now auto-detects out-of-office replies.

## Meeting Logistics

### Keep Initial Calls Short

- **15 minutes** for discovery/qualification
- Don't ask for 60-minute calls before you know if they have budget
- Short calls = less scary = more bookings

### Be Vague About Pricing

> "I'd want to understand your business first before giving you numbers. Happy to share a range on our call."

**Why:** 
- You might be able to charge more
- They might not have budget (call wouldn't help anyway)
- Shows you're consultative, not transactional

## Going the Extra Mile

**One of Instantly's best clients (insurance agency):**
- Targeted e-commerce stores
- Actually BOUGHT products from prospects
- Posted Instagram stories with the products
- Then reached out

Result: Massive response rates.

**If one client is worth $10K+ lifetime, this makes sense.**

## Converting Loom Viewers

If they watched the Loom but didn't reply:

**Follow-up:**
> "Hey [Name], just wanted to check — did you get a chance to watch the Loom? Happy to answer any questions or jump on a quick call to walk through it together."

If they're not watching Looms at all:
- Problem is in the email copy BEFORE the Loom
- Fix the email that introduces the Loom first
- Don't worry about Loom script until people are clicking

## Simplify Your Asks

**Too much:**
> "Here's the Loom link. Here's a case study. Here's my Calendly. Let me know!"

**Better:**
> "Here's the Loom. Let me know what you think — happy to chat more if it resonates."

One clear CTA per message.
