## Anchor-text variations (avoid repetition)

When linking to the same destination from multiple pieces, vary the anchor text — repeating "click here" or the exact page title weakens diversity signals. When you ship a piece, append 4-5 anchor variations for it here so future pieces can rotate them.

Example variations for a hypothetical `/features/twitter-monitoring`:
- "monitor Twitter mentions"
- "real-time Twitter monitoring"
- "Twitter mention tracking"
- "our Twitter monitoring tool"
