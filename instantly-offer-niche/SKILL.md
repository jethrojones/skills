# Instantly Offer & Niche Selection

Framework for selecting niches and crafting offers that get responses. Use the scorecard to evaluate before launching campaigns.

## The Offer Scorecard

Rate each factor for your offer. Higher total score = higher chance of success.

| Factor | Question | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Makes Money** | Does it directly increase revenue? | CEOs want to make more money |
| **Saves Time** | Does it reduce manual work? | Founders are busy |
| **Saves Money** | Is it cheaper than alternatives? | Easy ROI calculation |
| **Priority** | Is it on their to-do list NOW? | Only respond if it's urgent |
| **Multiple Providers** | Can they stack your service? | Easier to add than replace |
| **Fast Results** | Can you show results quickly? | "First ads up in 2 days" beats "results in 6 months" |
| **Clear ROI** | Can you quantify the return? | "Pay 10K, convert 2% more = 100K/year" |
| **Emerging + Proven** | Is there a new angle? | AI, automation = picks interest |
| **Domain Expertise** | Do you know something others don't? | Industry-specific knowledge wins |
| **Done For You** | Is it hands-off? | Busy founders prefer turnkey |
| **Performance Based** | Skin in the game? | Builds trust fast |

## Multiple Providers Concept

**Hard to sell (single provider):**
- SEO agency
- Facebook ads agency
- Design agency

**Easy to sell (stackable):**
- Lead generation (can always use more leads)
- Sales commission (more salespeople = more revenue)
- Content creation

If you're selling a "single provider" service, you MUST be clearly better than their current provider.

## The No-Brainer Offer Method

Start with the most irresistible offer possible, then work backwards to make it sustainable:

1. **Dream offer:** "As many clients as you can handle for no charge"
2. **Add commission:** "5 clients/week for 20% commission"
3. **Add specificity:** "5 sales meetings/week for 20% commission"
4. **Add guarantee:** "5 meetings/week or you don't pay"
5. **Add price:** "5 meetings/week for $250/meeting, guaranteed"

## Niching Down

**Generic (weak):**
> "We help e-commerce stores grow"

**Specific (strong):**
> "We help female founders in LA grow their sustainable makeup brands while putting the planet and people first"

| Variable | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Gender | Female founders |
| Position | Founders, CEOs, Marketing Directors |
| Location | LA, UK, Midlands |
| Sub-niche | Sustainable makeup (not just "beauty") |
| Value prop | Grow |
| Mission | Planet and people first |

**More variables = more personalization = more responses**

## Testing Approach

1. Break your services into separate offers
2. Score each with the scorecard above
3. Start with highest-scoring offer
4. Create 3 campaigns with different angles
5. Send 200-250 emails per campaign
6. Look for 1 positive response per 200 emails
7. If no responses, change targeting or copy

## Red Flags

- **Too broad:** "We do all your marketing"
- **No urgency:** "Rebranding" (situational, not priority)
- **No clear ROI:** "Better design" (how much money?)
- **Replacing existing:** Facebook ads for someone with an agency
